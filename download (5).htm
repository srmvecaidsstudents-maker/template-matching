Welcome to SRMVEC

Empowering Minds Engineering Futures

SRM Valliammai Engineering College, a division of the SRM Group of Educational Institutions, was established on 09.09.1999 under the Valliammai Educational Society with the vision “Educate to excel in social transformation,” and has been granted autonomous status by UGC, New Delhi and Anna University, Chennai.

Approved by AICTE and affiliated with Anna University, the college offers programs in Engineering & Technology, Management, and Computer Applications, including 11 undergraduate and 9 postgraduate courses.

Placement & Career Guidance

The Department of Placement and Career Guidance shoulders the responsibility of training the students and placing them in esteemed companies. Students from all the year and branches are trained for placement systematically to achieve the desired results.

The department meticulously works in networking with the companies and HRs of varied fields and schedules campus drives accordingly.

The department with gusto strives to cater and hone the students’ technical, communication and interview skills.

PROGRAMS OFFERED AT SRMVEC

Engineering a brighter future

Discover programs combining academics with hands-on learning exposure, preparing you for real-world careers.

  • Faculty of Engineering & Technology
  • Faculty of Management
  • Faculty of Science & Humanities
  • Faculty of Computer Applications

Department of Cyber Security

Bachelor of Cyber Security Engineering focuses on protecting digital systems, networks, and data from cyber threats and attacks. It provides fundamental technical knowledge in areas like network security, ethical hacking, cryptography, and risk management, preparing students to design and implement secure systems in real-world environments.

Department of Civil Engineering

Bachelor of Civil Engineering focuses on the design, construction, and maintenance of infrastructure like buildings, roads, and bridges. It provides fundamental technical knowledge for planning and executing civil engineering projects.

Department of Agricultural Engineering

Agricultural Engineering focuses on modernizing agriculture through advanced tools, systems, and sustainable practices.

Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science

Artificial Intelligence and Data Science combine machine learning, deep learning, and statistics to build intelligent systems that learn from large datasets and support smart decisions in real-world applications.

Department of Medical Electronics

Medical Electronics applies electronics and instrumentation to healthcare, designing and developing medical devices, diagnostic equipment, and health?monitoring systems that assist doctors in patient diagnosis and treatment.

Department of Computer Science and Engineering

Computer Science and Engineering focuses on designing and developing software, systems, and intelligent technologies.

Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering

Department of “Electronics and Communication Engineering” was established since inception of the college in the year 1999. National Board of Accreditation (NBA) has accredited the B.E-ECE programme for a period of 3 years. Initially, the annual intake was 60.

Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering

The Electrical and Electronics Engineering department of SRM Valliammai Engineering College was started in the year 2001. The Electrical and Electronics Engineering programme is duly approved by the AICTE and affiliated to ANNA University, Chennai.

Department of Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering (EIE)

The Department of Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering has been functioning since 2002, with a commitment to imparting quality education to undergraduate and postgraduate students in the field of Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering.

Department of Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Engineering focuses on the design, development, and maintenance of machines and mechanical systems.

Department of Information Technology

Information Technology (IT) is a field that focuses on the use of computers, software, networks, and systems to store, process, and manage data. It plays a key role in supporting communication, automation, and digital services across industries.

MBA – Master of Business Administration

MBA (Master of Business Administration) is a postgraduate program that focuses on developing skills in management, leadership, finance, marketing, and business strategy. It prepares students for managerial roles across various industries and enhances decision-making and problem-solving abilities.

English

The Department of English focuses on enhancing students’ communication and language skills through training in Listening, Speaking, Reading, and Writing (LSRW). It aims to build confidence, critical thinking, and professional competence, preparing students for academic success and industry readiness.

Mathematics

The Department of Mathematics provides a strong analytical foundation for students across all disciplines. It focuses on developing problem-solving skills, logical reasoning, and quantitative abilities essential for engineering, technology, and research. The curriculum bridges theoretical concepts with practical applications, enabling students to tackle real-world challenges effectively.

Physics

The Department of Physics provides a strong foundation in fundamental principles and their applications in engineering and technology. It focuses on developing analytical thinking, experimental skills, and scientific understanding among students, enabling them to apply physical concepts to real-world problems and emerging technologies.

Chemistry

“Transforming knowledge of chemistry into real-world impact.”

General Engineering

“Where engineering fundamentals shape future solutions.”

MCA - Master of Computer Applications

MCA (Master of Computer Applications) is a postgraduate program focused on advanced knowledge in computer applications, software development, programming, and IT systems. It prepares students for careers in software engineering, system management, and technology-driven industries.
